To capture a fresh screenshot of DesktopCleaner in action:
1. Prepare a folder with a mix of files in categories used by the tool.
2. Run `python desktop_cleaner.py --dry-run` to display the planned moves.
3. Use your operating system's screenshot tool to capture the terminal output.
4. Save the image as docs/screenshots/preview.png (do not commit the binary).
